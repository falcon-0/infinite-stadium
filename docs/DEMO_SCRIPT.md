## Proposed recording sequence

Keep the final video under two minutes. Rehearse the sequence against actual response times before recording; preserve genuine AI waits. If two questions do not fit, simplify the sequence instead of accelerating or fabricating an answer.

1. Select a synthetic fixture and press Play match. Show the moving pitch, action headline, score and compact statistics. Explain that the clubs/events are fictional and playback runs at the labelled 2× demo pace.
2. Ask a freely worded football question while playback continues. Show its pinned match time and actual answer. The answer should explain the moment using received evidence, including what the evidence cannot establish.
3. Apply a language, detail level or player focus in Viewing options. Show the resulting change; a selector alone does not demonstrate useful personalization. Include a real follow-up only if the complete response fits the recording.
4. Show a received correction in Watch and its effect on the current account or affected historical answer. Label any cut to a different match time. Later ordinary play does not invalidate a correctly pinned historical answer.
5. Open View recap after the passage ends. Identify recorded recap content accurately; show AI recap only if a real provider request was made and its output checked.
6. Close with the verified website and source links. Use readable captions and the approved sound treatment; no voice-over is required.

## Recording checks

Use the accepted frontend and validated backend release. Keep credentials, access codes and unrelated tabs out of frame. Capture actual app behavior, preserving the relationship between question, timestamp and answer. Never replace an unavailable response with scripted text or present editorial diagrams as functioning app footage.

Validate any displayed language answer for correctness; native-language quality remains unverified without a native reviewer. Recorded context, animation and recorded recaps are deterministic. Azure conversation uses model interpretation, application tools and review, but no general quality advantage over a simpler system has been established.

Twenty fixtures share five authored passage families; they are not twenty independent full-match simulations. The schematic display does not establish player intentions. Unobserved events remain unknown.

## Final gates

- [ ] Accepted Watch release identity recorded with the capture.
- [ ] Genuine current-release question captured while playback advances.
- [ ] Personalization and any language response checked.
- [ ] Watch correction and recorded recap checked on the same release.
- [ ] Video under two minutes, readable, owner-approved and publicly accessible on an accepted video platform.
- [ ] Public repository, private judge access instructions and judging-period provider availability verified.
- [ ] Fan findings included only when actual participants supplied them.
